A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

An estimate to
grow from.

CO₂e means “carbon dioxide equivalent”—a shared unit for the warming impact of different greenhouse gases. This calculator follows the supplied Hydroponic Carbon Footprint Calculator workbook.

It covers purchased electricity, water, nutrients, growing media, packaging, farm-related travel and annualized equipment. Building construction and employee commuting are outside this estimate.

This is a screening estimate, not a full life-cycle assessment. Local energy and supplier data will make it more relevant to your grow.
